Privacy

Updated November 24, 2026

Laïyana collects nothing about you. No account, no ads, no analytics, nothing sent without you knowing.

What stays on your computer

Your collection, plays, playlists, favorites, lyrics and settings are kept in Laïyana's data folder, on your PC. They never leave it, unless you make a backup yourself (Settings › My data).

What goes online

Nothing by default. Every exchange with the internet depends on a setting you turn on yourself, and can turn off at any time (Settings › Online). There are no analytics, in any case.

Look online off by default

To find artist photos, biographies, missing covers and credits, Laïyana sends the artist's name and the album or track title, and for credits the track's length, to these open services:

  • MusicBrainz and Cover Art Archive: credits and covers;
  • Wikidata, Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons: artist photos and biographies.

Online lyrics off by default

To find the lyrics of a track that has none on your drive, Laïyana sends the artist, title, album and length of the track to LRCLIB, and failing that to lyrics.ovh.

Last.fm off by default

If you connect your Last.fm account (Settings › Online), Laïyana sends it, for each track played past its halfway point, the artist, title, album, album artist, length and time played, as well as the track now playing. When you connect, your plays from the last 14 days are sent too. Never a file name. The session key is kept in Windows Credential Manager; “Disconnect” erases it. What Last.fm does with these plays falls under its own privacy policy.

Radio and podcasts off by default, in trial

These two tabs are in trial and will arrive in an update. If you turn them on:

  • the station directory comes from Radio Browser, and your station search is sent to it;
  • your podcast search is sent to Apple's (iTunes) directory;
  • the audio, the episode list and the images come straight from each station's and each podcast's own servers;
  • for Radio France stations, the current title is read from api.radiofrance.fr.

Playing over your local network

When you send music to a device at home (DLNA player, Chromecast), Laïyana talks to it directly: the track, its title, artist and album go to that device. Nothing leaves your local network.

In every case, no identifier, file name or path on your drive is ever sent to an online service. As with any website, the services contacted see your IP address; their own privacy policies apply.

Problem reports

Ctrl + Shift + R copies a technical report (the open page, playback, audio output, errors) to your clipboard. It goes nowhere: you choose whether to paste it, for example into a GitHub issue.

The Microsoft Store

If you install Laïyana from the Microsoft Store, installation and updates go through Microsoft, under its own privacy statement.

Your data, your rights

Since nothing is collected, there is nothing to delete on our side. To remove your data, uninstall Laïyana or delete its data folder. Any question: contact@laiyana.app.
