    Questions

    Do I need a DAC?

    No. In studio mode, Laïyana plays bit-perfect on your computer’s sound card. A DAC and good headphones let you hear everything your files hold, especially in Hi-Res.

    What is the difference between WASAPI exclusive and ASIO?

    Both bypass the Windows mixer. WASAPI exclusive works with every card; ASIO goes through your DAC maker’s driver.

    Does my collection stay on my PC?

    Yes. Laïyana reads your folders, moves nothing without asking, and never sends it anywhere. Online lookups (artist info, lyrics) are off by default, need no account and only send names and titles.

    Where does Laïyana find booklets?

    With your music, with nothing else to install: the scans in the album folder and its image subfolders (Scans, Artwork, Booklet…), in name order, and PDF booklets, page by page. The cover alone does not count as a booklet. An album preview shows its page count, the grid has a “With booklet” filter, and you leaf through them full size in Sleeve.

    What about Mac or Linux?

    Not yet: Laïyana is made for Windows first, where it talks straight to your sound card. A Mac version will come later, funded by Pro (I’ll need a Mac to build it). No date promised.

    What’s the catch?

    Laïyana is free, no account, no ads. An optional Pro will come later (headphone correction, quality check, player sync). Nothing that’s free today will ever move to Pro.

    I just want to play a file.

    Open a file, it plays. Sort your music whenever you like: double-click a track or drop it on the window, and Laïyana plays it right away, nothing to sort first. Your library can wait.

    I found a bug.

    Press Ctrl + Shift + R in the app: a report is copied. Paste it into an issue on GitHub, or email it to contact@laiyana.app.
